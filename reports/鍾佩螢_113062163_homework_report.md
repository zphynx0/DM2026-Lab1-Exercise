The dataset contains 813 unique documents after duplicates were dropped (`check_duplicates_4`), no missing text was found (`check_missing_3`). The documents are short social-media-style posts labeled -1.0, 0.0 and 1.0. A document-term matrix was also built (`dtm_7`) with a vocabulary of 4,313 terms.

The variance filter (`variance_filter_11`, threshold=0.15) kept only 32/4,313 terms. The highest-variance terms were all common function words: "the" (5.13), "to" (2.18), "and" (1.78), "is" (1.06) and "you" (0.98). The removed terms all had a variance of 0.001228, which means that they appeared in only 1 document. The Pearson filter (`pearson_filter_13`, target class=1.0, threshold=0.1) kept 41 terms. The strongest correlations were weak (the largest is 0.156, for "huge", "main" and "tldr"), followed by "nice" (0.156) and "technology" (0.152). Several terms share the exact same value, for example 0.128622 for "changing", "god", "major", "perfectly" and "points". This suggests these words appear in very few documents, so the correlations may not be reliable. The bar chart of the 20 strongest terms is in `plots\鍾佩螢_homework_plot_turn14.png`. The correlation heatmap of the 20 highest-variance terms (`feature_corr_14`, `plots\鍾佩螢_homework_plot_feature_corr_14.png`) shows positive relationship between common words, such as "the"–"in" (0.692), "the"–"and" (0.659) and "will"–"be" (0.591). "he" is almost uncorrelated with the rest (-0.031).

Pattern mining on class 1.0 used FP-Growth with the term-frequency filter, which kept 977 terms. No patterns were found at `min_sup` of 20 (`patterns_16`), 10 (`patterns_17`) and 5 (`patterns_18`), and `min_sup=1` ran out of memory. Short posts share very few words, and after stop-word removal no remaining word appears in even 5 documents of this class. This may reflect the short length of the posts and the limited word overlap after filtering.

PCA reduced the matrix to 2D (`reduce_dimensions_19`, `plots\鍾佩螢_homework_plot_reduce_dimensions_19.png`). PC1 explains 26.7% of the variance and PC2 explains 5.12%, so the 2 combined capture about 31.8%. This means the 2 dimensions capture only part of the data's variation. In the plot, the 3 classes overlapped heavily in 1 dense cluster, so PCA doesn't separate them by label. A few outliers appeared in both directions, but they come from all 3 classes. This explains why the explained variance is low and the classes are not clearly separated in the 2D plot.

Label binarization (`binarize_labels_20`) one-hot encoded the three classes into an 813 × 3 matrix. The cosine similarity between documents 0 and 1 (`cosine_sim_21`) was 0.0, this means that the 2 posts shared no terms in the vocabulary. This is common because posts are short. A score of 0.0 means there are no shared words, but it doesn't necessarily mean the posts have different meanings.
